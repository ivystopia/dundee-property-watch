About this archive and its map data

Each entry records the price and availability on the day it was reported. An older entry for the same home stays in the archive after a price change. Check the listing for current availability.

“Listed” is the date supplied by the listing. “First seen” is the date the watch found the home when the original listing date was unavailable.

Neighbourhood names come from listing descriptions or local geographic evidence. A statistical area or postcode locality gives wider context when a neighbourhood is unconfirmed. Walking times and distances follow mapped pedestrian routes from Tip Top at a typical pace. Listing map pins, street positions and postcode centres are approximate.

Walking routes use Valhalla. Postcode routes use the Geofabrik Scotland extract of OpenStreetMap and Mapzen/Tilezen terrain data. Area and postcode references include Scottish Government boundaries, Public Health Scotland geography names, Dundee City Council Street Finder, Check My Postcode and OS Open Names.
